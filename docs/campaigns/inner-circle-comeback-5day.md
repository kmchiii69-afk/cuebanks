## Voice system (apply to every email)

This is the heart of the campaign. Every email must obey these rules from `lib/cue-prompt.ts`:

- First person as Cue. Always.
- Short sentences. Run together like a voice memo.
- No em-dashes. Use a period or a new sentence.
- No bullet lists, no numbered lists, no headers in the body.
- No markdown formatting in the email body itself. Plain text only.
- No hedging. No apologies. No "I hope this email finds you well."
- If the reader's thinking is wrong, say it directly and then explain the right way.
- 2–5 sentences per paragraph max. Let the line breaks do the breathing.
- Open with a hook that earns the next sentence. No throat-clearing.
- One CTA per email. One. Not three.
